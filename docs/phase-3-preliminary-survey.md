# Phase 3: Stakeholder Interaction & Preliminary Survey

## 1. Survey Overview
To understand the community's awareness and pain points regarding renewable energy, the team conducted a preliminary survey and a series of in-person stakeholder interviews.

- **Location:** AECS Layout, Bangalore
- **Target Audience:** Local residents, homeowners, and small business owners.
- **Methodology:** 
  - Online Google Form distributed via local community WhatsApp groups (50 responses).
  - In-person structured interviews (5 key stakeholders).

## 2. In-Person Interview Records

### Interviewee 1: Mr. Ramesh (Homeowner, AECS Layout)
- **Current Energy Source:** 100% BESCOM grid.
- **Interest in Solar:** High, but hesitant due to the upfront cost.
- **Key Pain Point:** "I want to install rooftop solar, but I don't know who the trusted vendors are in Bangalore, or how to calculate the ROI (Return on Investment)."

### Interviewee 2: Mrs. Anitha (Small Bakery Owner)
- **Current Energy Source:** BESCOM grid + Diesel Generator for backup.
- **Interest in Renewable Backup:** Moderate.
- **Key Pain Point:** "My commercial electricity bills are very high. I don't know if solar panels can support my heavy equipment or how much space they require."

## 3. Online Survey Findings (Summary of 50 Responses)
1. **General Awareness:** 85% of respondents are aware of renewable energy, but only 12% currently use any form of it at home (mostly solar water heaters).
2. **Biggest Barriers to Entry:** 
   - 60% cited "High initial setup cost."
   - 25% cited "Lack of reliable information or trusted vendors."
   - 15% cited "Living in rented apartments / lack of roof space."
3. **Desire for Digital Tools:** 90% of respondents stated they would actively use a free online portal to estimate their potential savings and read simplified guides on sustainable energy.

## 4. Conclusion & Impact on Project Scope
The preliminary survey confirms that while the AECS Layout community is environmentally conscious, there is a severe lack of accessible, localized, and trusted information. 

**Actionable Takeaway for the C.R.E.A.M Portal:** 
Based on stakeholder feedback, our portal *must* prioritize an **Energy Calculator** feature to help users visualize savings, alongside an **Educational Module** to simplify the transition to sustainable energy practices.
