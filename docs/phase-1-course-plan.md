## 🎯 Course Objectives & Ethics Statement
As a team, we understand that the Societal Project builds awareness on **Educational Social Responsibility (ESR)**. 

**Our Project Goals (SDGs 7, 11, 15):**
1. Design and develop a web portal providing information on renewable energy resources.
2. Promote the adoption of sustainable energy practices within the community.
3. Solve real issues by adapting technology for societal development.

**Code of Conduct:**
We commit to maintaining professionalism, ethical conduct, and respect while interacting with community stakeholders. Our project will remain academic, socially responsible, technically focused, and politically neutral, considering practical constraints such as cost, resources, and social impact.
