# Phase 5: Literature Review & Baseline Analysis

## 1. Introduction
Before developing the C.R.E.A.M Portal, the team conducted a baseline analysis of existing solutions in the renewable energy space. The goal was to identify gaps in current tools and ensure our portal provides unique, localized value to the community.

## 2. Review of Existing Solutions

### A. Government Portals (e.g., MNRE Solar Calculator)
- **Overview:** Official calculators provided by government bodies to estimate solar rooftop capacity.
- **Strengths:** Highly accurate data regarding government subsidies and technical irradiation values.
- **Weaknesses:** The User Interface (UI) is often outdated and overly technical for the average homeowner. It lacks educational context and a modern, engaging dashboard.

### B. Private Vendor Apps (e.g., Tata Power Solar, Luminous)
- **Overview:** Applications provided by specific solar hardware manufacturing companies.
- **Strengths:** Streamlined process for purchasing equipment and getting direct installation quotes.
- **Weaknesses:** Highly commercialized. These apps are locked into their own ecosystems and do not provide neutral, unbiased educational content on general energy conservation.

### C. Generic Energy Tracking Apps
- **Overview:** Mobile apps that allow users to manually log their electricity bills.
- **Strengths:** Good for tracking financial expenses month-over-month.
- **Weaknesses:** They rarely include ROI calculators for renewable energy transition and completely lack community-focused awareness features.

## 3. Baseline Analysis & Comparative Advantage
Our baseline analysis reveals that while technical calculators and commercial vendor apps exist, there is a missing "middle ground"—a platform that is non-commercial, highly educational, and easy for everyday users to understand.

**How C.R.E.A.M Portal Bridges the Gap:**
1. **Neutrality:** Unlike vendor apps, our portal provides unbiased educational content on various sustainable practices (not just trying to sell panels).
2. **Simplicity:** We abstract the complex mathematics of solar sizing into an intuitive, user-friendly UI.
3. **Community Focus:** Designed specifically for localized community awareness and education rather than national-scale hardware sales.

## 4. Conclusion
The literature review confirms the necessity of the C.R.E.A.M Portal. Existing solutions are either too technical, too commercial, or too generic. Our project will successfully fill this niche by providing an accessible, educational, and engaging platform tailored for local community empowerment.
