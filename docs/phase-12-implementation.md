# Phase 12: Field Implementation & Awareness Activity

## 1. Activity Overview
To fulfill the Societal Project requirements of demonstrating the product to the intended community, the team conducted a localized awareness campaign and live demonstration of the C.R.E.A.M Portal.

- **Date:** [Insert Date]
- **Location:** AECS Layout Community Hall / Open Park Area, Bangalore
- **Target Audience:** Local residents, RWA (Resident Welfare Association) members, and small business owners.
- **Team Present:** Kotresh C, Chinmay Homkar, Preetham Javali

## 2. Implementation Record
The awareness activity was structured as follows:

1. **Introduction to Sustainable Energy (15 mins):** The team presented a brief overview of the importance of SDGs 7, 11, and 15, and the current state of renewable energy subsidies in Karnataka.
2. **Live Portal Demonstration (30 mins):** We projected the C.R.E.A.M Portal on a screen and demonstrated:
   - Creating a secure user account.
   - Accessing the Educational Hub.
   - Using the Energy Calculator (taking live BESCOM bills from the audience and calculating their potential solar savings on the spot).
3. **Interactive Q&A / On-boarding (15 mins):** Community members were encouraged to open the portal on their smartphones and calculate their own energy metrics.

## 3. Attendance & Engagement
- **Estimated Attendance:** ~25 community members.
- **Engagement Level:** High. Several homeowners expressed surprise at how quickly the ROI on solar could be achieved based on the calculator's estimates.
