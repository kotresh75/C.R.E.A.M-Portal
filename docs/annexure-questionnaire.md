# Annexure A: Preliminary Survey Questionnaire

**Target Audience:** Local residents and small business owners in AECS Layout.
**Method:** Online Google Form and In-Person Structured Interviews.

1. **What is your primary source of electricity for your home/business?**
   - [ ] BESCOM Grid only
   - [ ] BESCOM Grid + Diesel Backup
   - [ ] Renewable (Solar/Wind) + Grid
2. **How would you rate your awareness of the long-term financial benefits of installing rooftop solar panels?**
   - [ ] Very High (I know exactly how much I would save)
   - [ ] Moderate (I know it saves money, but not sure how much)
   - [ ] Low (I have no idea)
3. **What is the biggest barrier preventing you from transitioning to renewable energy? (Select one)**
   - [ ] High initial setup cost
   - [ ] Lack of reliable information or trusted vendors
   - [ ] Living in a rented apartment / lack of roof space
   - [ ] Too complicated to understand
4. **Do you currently know how to calculate the Return on Investment (ROI) for a solar installation based on your monthly electricity bill?**
   - [ ] Yes
   - [ ] No
5. **If a free, unbiased online tool existed to calculate your exact solar requirements and financial savings in seconds, how likely are you to use it?**
   - [ ] Highly Likely
   - [ ] Somewhat Likely
   - [ ] Unlikely
