# Phase 2: Community Problem Identification & Topic Selection

## 1. Problem Identification
Through discussion and observation of local communities, we identified a significant gap in awareness and management of renewable energy resources. Many individuals and local organizations are unaware of the benefits of renewable energy (such as solar, wind, and biogas), how to transition to sustainable practices, or how to calculate their current energy footprint and potential savings. 

## 2. Shortlisted Project Topic
**Project Title:** Community Renewable Energy Awareness and Management Portal (C.R.E.A.M)

**Broad Area:** Renewable Energy Education, Sustainability, and Civic Technology.

## 3. Measurable Engineering Outcome
Design and develop a comprehensive web portal to provide actionable information on renewable energy resources, integrate tools for tracking energy usage, and actively promote the adoption of sustainable energy practices within the community.

## 4. Alignment with Educational Standards
- **Specific POs (Program Outcomes):** 6 (The Engineer and Society: Apply reasoning informed by contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to professional engineering practice).
- **SDGs (Sustainable Development Goals):**
  - **Goal 7:** Affordable and Clean Energy
  - **Goal 11:** Sustainable Cities and Communities
  - **Goal 15:** Life on Land

## 5. Feasibility Summary
The project is technically and practically feasible. The team possesses the required Full Stack Development skills (React, FastAPI, MongoDB) to build the portal. The scope is well-defined, and the solution can be fully implemented, tested, and demonstrated to stakeholders within the 15-week semester timeline without requiring excessive financial resources.
